    What it does

    Everything a teacher needs to read the room

    Live confusion gauge

    Students tap Got it, Unsure or Lost. The gauge and timeline show how the room is following, minute by minute.

    Anonymous doubts

    Doubts arrive without names. Classmates tap Me too, so the most-shared questions rise to the top.

    AI themes

    Claude groups scattered doubts into a few themes, ranks them by students affected and suggests how to explain each one again.

    Quick checks

    Send a 3-question check aimed at what the class is stuck on. Results stay hidden on your screen until you reveal them.

    Class report

    End the class for the numbers, the themes, a plan for the next lecture and practice questions for students.

    Google Meet companion

    Attach your Meet link, float the live meter above the call, or present the pulse to everyone in the meeting.

    How a class runs

    Four steps, no installs

    1. Start a class and put the join code on the projector or in your Meet call.

    2. Students join on their phones, tap Got it, Unsure or Lost, and send doubts.

    3. Read the room. Find themes, then send a 3-question check to every phone.

    4. End the class for a report with next steps and practice questions.
